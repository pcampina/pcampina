## 🧭 How I work

Owning a product alone means owning every decision: scope, UX, architecture, pricing, release and support. That is the lens I bring to engineering teams.

- **Frontend architecture that scales with the team:** monorepos (Turborepo), design systems, clear module boundaries, SOLID
- **Product engineering:** ship the smallest thing that solves the real problem, then iterate on what users actually do
- **Cross-stack delivery:** web, native Apple platforms and audio, from the first commit to production
- **AI as a multiplier:** AI agents speed up delivery without replacing engineering judgment or UX quality
